Each criterion is a `### key` heading (lowercase latin letters, digits, `_`) followed by a plain-language
description: what matters, what counts as a failure, where to look for evidence (amenities, house rules,
reviews, surroundings). Order = priority: the first one matters most. By default a `fail` on any of the
first three criteria removes the listing (see `hard_criteria` / `must_pass` in config.yaml).

### quiet_sleep
Quiet at night: bedroom windows not on a busy street or above a bar; no complaints about noise,
construction or thin walls. Fail: repeated complaints about night-time noise.

### real_cleanliness
Clean in practice, not just in the description: no complaints about dirt, mould, insects or smells.
Fail: two or more recent reviews about dirt or pests.

### food_nearby
Several places to eat within 15 minutes by car (surroundings: `food`). Fail: fewer than two.

### working_ac
Air conditioning in every bedroom and it actually cools (reviews saying "AC didn't cool" are a warning).
